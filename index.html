Who We Are

A medical center built around the patient

Located on the main road of Samqaniyeh, Chouf, next to Al Makhazen Al Koubra, the center offers comprehensive healthcare through a specialized team covering general and mental health, pediatrics, women's health and fertility, orthopedic surgery, clinical nutrition, general and cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, dental implants, oral and maxillofacial surgery, and early childhood development — in a modern environment focused on families across Chouf and all of Lebanon.
