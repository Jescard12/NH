About Us

Healthcare built on trust

Dr. Nasser Abou Hamdan Specialized Medical Center is a comprehensive healthcare destination bringing together doctors and therapists from multiple specialties, in a modern, patient-first environment.

Our Mission

Personalized, evidence-based care

Located on the main road of Samqaniyeh, Chouf, next to Al Makhazen Al Koubra, the center brings together a multidisciplinary team covering physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, psychology and psychiatry, pediatric and newborn care, women's health and fertility, orthopedic surgery, clinical nutrition, general, cosmetic and implant dentistry with orthodontics, oral, maxillofacial and plastic surgery, and early childhood development. We are committed to personalized, evidence-based healthcare in a modern, patient-centered environment serving families across Chouf and all of Lebanon.
